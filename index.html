02 / OUR STORY

A HUNDRED YEARS IN THE MAKING

From Victor
to Stilco.

It began in 1925, in Lohar Chawl.

Our great-grandfather built a manufacturing business around stoves and Petromax lamps under the Victor name. The products travelled across India and Pakistan, earning a place in homes at a time when reliable light and heat mattered every day.

As electricity changed the way people lived, the next generation adapted. Our father and uncles carried the business forward as retailers, suppliers and distributors.

Joining the trade in 1997, we learned it from the ground up: from customers, from the market, and from the guidance of the generation before us. In April 2002, a defining personal turning point changed the direction of the business. Step by step, we expanded into the supply and distribution networks of established companies.

Today, the products and the business have evolved, but the principle remains the same: trust is earned over time, and every customer relationship matters.
